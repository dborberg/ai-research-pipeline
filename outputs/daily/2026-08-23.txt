<h3>TOP THEME OF THE DAY</h3>
<p>The day’s dominant theme is that AI investment is becoming less about model enthusiasm and more about funding capacity, infrastructure permission, and production readiness. For advisor conversations, the useful frame is that the AI cycle still has powerful demand signals, but returns will increasingly depend on capital discipline, local bottlenecks, governance controls, and the ability to turn AI spending into durable business productivity.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Alibaba prepared a major AI financing:</strong> Alibaba reportedly plans a $10 billion Hong Kong share placement to help fund AI spending, underscoring how large platform companies are turning to external capital to support AI buildouts. The advisor read-through is that AI remains a strategic priority in China’s technology sector, but the investment conversation should include dilution, capital intensity, and the timing of eventual monetization. (Source: Reuters)</li>
<li><strong>AI data center opposition escalated at the local level:</strong> Local officials have reportedly faced death threats and gunfire tied to AI data center projects, while more than 500 towns have restricted builds and some councils have shut down public comment. (Source: Tom's Hardware)</li>
</ul>

<h3>TOP THEME OF THE DAY</h3>
<p>Today’s strongest signal is that AI competition is moving deeper into infrastructure, local policy, and platform distribution rather than staying confined to model launches. For advisor conversations, the investable AI cycle increasingly depends on who can secure power, data-center capacity, compliant market access, and trusted enterprise controls while avoiding bottlenecks from communities, regulators, and capital discipline.</p>
